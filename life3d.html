    How to Play DEMIURGE

    Goal: Guide your character to live as long and successfully as possible.

    Core Stats

    • 💪 Strength: Affects physical abilities and lifespan. Increase it by working out.
    • 🧠 Intelligence: Affects learning, income, and problem-solving. Increase it by training.
    • 💰 Coins: The currency used to buy items and services.
    • AGE: Your character's current age.
    • Lifespan: The age your character is expected to live to. This can change based on your actions.

    Careers

    Once you meet the stat and education requirements, you can choose a career to earn passive income. Better careers require higher stats and education.

    Education

    Enroll in school to permanently increase your intelligence and unlock advanced careers. Education costs coins and takes time (game ticks).

    Actions

    Perform actions like "Feed" or "Train" to manage your character's stats. Some actions have a coin cost or a cooldown period.

    Store

    Buy items from the Idol Store to get powerful buffs that can increase your stats or provide other benefits.
